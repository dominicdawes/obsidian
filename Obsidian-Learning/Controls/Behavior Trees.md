
### Overview

- Structure: BTs are hierarchical structures composed of nodes (actions, conditions, selectors, sequences, etc.) that represent different behaviors.
- Execution: The BT is executed top-down, with the execution flow determined by the outcomes of the nodes.
- Pros: Highly flexible and modular, can represent complex behaviors with ease, supports parallel execution of tasks.
- Cons: Can be more challenging to understand and debug compared to FSMs for complex systems.


### Relationship and Use Cases

- **Complementary:** FSMs and BTs can be used together to complement each other's strengths. For example, FSMs can be used to represent low-level, sequential behaviors within a larger BT.
